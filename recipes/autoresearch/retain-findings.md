# Retain findings and their provenance

Subrecipe of [Autoresearch](../autoresearch.md). Retain every disposition, including
negative, invalid and inconclusive evidence, without converting a claim into proof.

## Inputs

The plan/governance/recipe closure, environment manifest, run receipts and outputs,
evaluation, applicable review policy and private/public retention boundaries.

## Steps

1. Verify exact source and artifact references, hashes, producer identity and
   observation basis. Keep unknown attribution, incomplete coverage and unavailable
   signatures explicit. Signed prose alone does not verify external artifact bytes.
2. Retain private raw outputs/receipts and bounded immutable inventories. Keep
   credentials, raw harness transcripts and bulky captures out of source commits
   and public summaries. Record authorized omissions and reconstruction gaps.
3. Write the research finding through the selected KB tool/provider. Link question,
   decisions, invariants, recipe versions, parent/child runs, data, evaluator and
   verdict. Distinguish observation, interpretation, supported conclusion and open
   question. Record failure/rejection and the premise that would justify revisiting.
4. Use task/report lifecycle for assigned work and review; use artifact claims for
   responsibility without treating a claim as verification. Preserve original
   operation IDs on uncertain writes, and query their outcomes before retrying.
5. Checkpoint native KB documents with its supported commit tool at a substantial
   handoff. Publishing canonical source recipes/artifacts uses the repository's
   signed source/landing workflow; never add private host records to source Git or
   claim that a source commit checkpointed native drafts.
6. Feed reviewed evidence to Dream only through the supported source/trust/trial/
   publication boundary. Preserve applicability and revisit conditions. A useful
   scientific result does not by itself pass a same-entry/harness memory trial or
   grant new execution, trust-policy or instruction authority.

## Outputs and verification

Return exact KB references/revisions and immutable artifact locators, actual write/
checkpoint outcomes, review state and remaining gaps. Read back the complete result
with stable etag pagination and compare expected content. A stored write is not
proof that another model read or applied it, and a memory offer is not proven benefit.

## Failure and recovery

Preserve the private evidence and original pending operation on KB/identity/provider
failure. Do not invoke retired providers, invent pins, duplicate unknown effects or
bypass a missing KB tool with filesystem writes. Follow the declared per-program
review-backlog limit while independent work proceeds. Publication remains a separate
authorized effect with its own audience and verification.
