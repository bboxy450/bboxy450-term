# Choose the next bounded research step

Subrecipe of [Autoresearch](../autoresearch.md). Convert dispositioned evidence
into a changed question, another bounded test or an honest stop.

## Inputs

Retained findings and review state; current governing decisions/invariants;
remaining parent budget, deadlines and leases; declared novelty, review and stop
conditions; outstanding original operation IDs.

## Steps

1. Integrate all required companion/control outcomes and named gaps. Do not select
   only the improving arm or treat overlapping labels as independent trials.
2. State what changed: a new observation, falsified prediction, altered premise,
   justified intervention or a dependency becoming available. Repeating an identical
   refusal is not progress. Keep informative failures and unexpected directions.
3. Recheck applicable decisions and their supersession, invariant checks, scope and
   budget. Changed policy is an explicit revision for future admissions, never a
   retroactive rewrite of completed evidence. A recipe cannot amend decisions or
   weaken an invariant to continue itself.
4. If the question is resolved, the budget/deadline expired, required evidence is
   unavailable or a declared stall/review rule fires, close this invocation with
   its disposition and useful revisit condition. Do not silently renew a lease,
   extend a spent attempt or require the operator to keep an unattended test alive.
5. Otherwise select a new bounded question/experiment within the admitted program.
   Pin its applicable recipe/policy closure and input/environment use, then return
   the choice to the parent. Its next bounded iteration invokes
   [Propose experiment](propose-experiment.md). Hypotheses and explanatory results
   are valid; a scalar incumbent is optional and governed by its declared rule.
6. Persist next action/due trigger and original parent/child IDs before handing off
   to the existing harness/driver. Notifications do not drive the iteration clock.
   Preserve outstanding/unknown effects for reconciliation on restart.
7. Release only the invocation's owned resources after actual settlement, or retain
   the declared reusable lease/quarantine and expiry evidence. Do not stop another
   program or hold a global queue while this one changes direction.

## Outputs and verification

Return the new experiment/protocol reference or a terminal disposition, why it
follows from retained evidence, remaining budget and cleanup/recovery state. A fresh
run identity cannot reset the parent's cumulative allowance. Record any proposed
decision/spec change separately for the appropriate authority; it does not apply
until actually adopted under current policy.

## Failure and recovery

A missing decision, contradictory invariant, unresolved effect or exhausted budget
is retained as an explicit gap/disposition for this invocation. Preserve enough
state to resume/revisit lawfully; do not duplicate work, manufacture novelty or park
the whole swarm on one person, resource or model conversation.
