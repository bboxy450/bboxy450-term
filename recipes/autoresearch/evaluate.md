# Evaluate retained experiment evidence

Subrecipe of [Autoresearch](../autoresearch.md). Produce a scoped verdict from a
fixed evaluator and matching settled evidence. A deterministic scorer needs no
additional model or human judge.

## Inputs

The frozen plan and selection/confirmation rule; applicable decisions/invariants;
settled receipts and exact artifact/environment identities; fixed evaluator and
its declared input/partition access; remaining evaluation budget.

## Steps

1. Verify receipt and artifact closure, source/input/evaluator and material
   environment compatibility. Preserve the distinction between observed identity,
   preflight hashes, reported claims and unsupported proof. Unsettled or mismatched
   evidence cannot justify promotion.
2. Apply the fixed scoring/confirmation command or supported adapter under its
   admitted effect/isolation profile. Candidate stdout is data; the scorer owns the
   authoritative result channel. Enforced held-out/evaluator protection requires
   an actually supported backend, not merely a private directory or tool label.
3. Check baselines, controls, repeated samples/seeds, invalid/unmatched cases,
   coverage, unknown outcomes, costs and uncertainty under the declared protocol.
   Inspection evaluates committed predictions; intervention evaluates its measured
   effect and controls. A plausible explanation is not a demonstrated mechanism.
4. Emit measured metrics or checked predictions with units/direction, sample
   counts, exclusions, uncertainty, exact evidence and a scoped disposition:
   supported/rejected/inconclusive claim, improved/rejected candidate, or invalid/
   crashed/incomplete execution. Do not turn missing evidence into zero or success.
5. Run the applicable post-settlement invariants and retain actual exits. A new
   code/protocol revision needs fresh relevant evidence. Reusing source-bound
   checks is valid only within their recorded scope.
6. If selection is authorized, advance the incumbent by the declared comparison
   rule against its expected revision and exact candidate/evidence. A late result
   cannot promote across a changed policy/input/evaluator or A -> B -> A. A better
   development score may only nominate fresh confirmation where the domain requires
   it; Winnow's paper evidence establishes no repeatable profit or live-order authority.

## Outputs and verification

Return an evidence-bound evaluation artifact, check results and selection outcome
or explicit reason for no selection. Record effects on other tasks/controls and
remaining explanatory/generalization limits. Task acceptance separately verifies
that the assigned work was correctly performed; a losing candidate can be valid work.

## Failure and recovery

Retain evaluator failures, unsupported comparisons, contamination and uncertainty.
Do not revise the evaluator to obtain a pass, erase a crash, leak final confirmation
into the active search or let the proposer approve its own claim through prose.
An evaluation redesign belongs to a new protocol revision with retained rationale.
