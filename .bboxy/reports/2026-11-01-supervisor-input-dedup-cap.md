## Formal bounds and deliberate limits

The supervisor actor owns five model variables: four-admission count, oldest/newest IDs and their receipt states (vacant/Pending/PtyWritten/PartialUnknown), initially zero. Two slots abstract 4096 production receipts. Admission plus eviction is atomic; write or timeout is separate and may be delayed. Retained replay, conflict and Busy are nonmutating actions. Weak fairness is assumed for fresh admission and the completion/timeout group; successful writes are not assumed. Safety keeps only the latest two admitted IDs and at most one pending write; liveness admits the fourth input beyond capacity and eventually terminates each pending receipt. Broken removes only eviction and must reach a permanently full terminal map that fails liveness. Existing InputReceipt covers lease fencing/at-most-once effects while retained; it is not a lifetime promise after eviction.

The model does not prove unbounded Rust refinement, byte hashes, actual PTY/OS durability, physical deadlines or the diagnostics sink. Real PTY, ownership and private journal witnesses cover selected implementation behavior. Evicted-ID reuse is intentionally a new operation; PartialUnknown is never automatically replayed. No operator desktop, credentials, installed app or live stuck-seat recovery was used. UI geometry/parity is unchanged.
