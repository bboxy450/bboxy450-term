# Decision 53: the two first mantras

The operator ranked both "#1" on 2026-10-23, in that order. They are two sides of one thing: you can only iterate fast if iterating costs nothing.

**Be flexible and always evolve.** Improve, iterate, experiment, test the experience, discard or adopt. The practice already runs: Wren builds variants in the lab, the operator picks by eye, the pick becomes a decision with its words, native pairs with it under strict parity, and what was not picked is kept as history and never promoted ([[rim-glow]] tonight: inside rim, then four pixels, then half and half, then the swell, in one evening). The RFC loop ([[work-items]]) is the same shape for ideas on paper. Judgement, not a gate: the measure is how long an experiment stays undecided, and the Oracle's ZOOM OUT reads it.

**A restart loses nothing.** Fast reload of a native GPU app without losing working state, because no working state exists only in process memory: seats in the supervisor (#11), layout and history in the account journal and the ring, read marks in the account object, tasks and every record in frontmatter the operator can open and edit. "State lives in frontmatter that the user sees" is the design rule; the invariant [[restart-keeps-state]] is its gate, pending the test that relaunches the whole working set (RS1). Rejected: in-memory caches as the source of any state the operator would miss; hot-reload machinery that bypasses the store.
