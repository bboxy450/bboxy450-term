# Permissive SVG admission

**Date:** 2026-11-24. **Decided by:** operator, implemented by Oracle Vesper Greywind (seat 01M8764D).

**Operator:** "if other renderer accepts SVG we treat it as renderable, and permissive, lets be more permissive, lets make it more comfortable for harnesses to render SVGs here".

## Decision
`bboxy450-svg` admission changes from a closed refuse-anything-unknown subset to browser-style best effort:
- Unknown elements, attributes, CSS properties and foreign namespaces are ignored, not refused.
- Basic shapes `circle`, `ellipse`, `polygon`, `polyline` and rounded `rect` (`rx`/`ry`) lower to paths. `a`, `switch` and `use` of a `symbol` render as groups.
- `<style>` rules with simple selectors (`*`, `tag`, `.class`, `#id`, `tag.class`) apply. Other selectors are ignored. `!important` is stripped.
- `currentColor`, `url(#id) fallback`, and invalid paint fall back to the property's initial paint. A missing clip target is ignored.
- Gradients the painter cannot draw exactly (radial, user-space, transformed, translucent or malformed stops) paint their first stop as a solid.
- Text renders with the bundled faces whatever its family. Weight is mapped to 400 or 700. A missing `data-bboxy-font-set` is admitted.
- Lengths accept em/ex/in/cm/mm/pc. Percentages and unknown units fall back to the viewBox size. Opacity is clamped. Unparsable transforms are ignored.

## Still refused (safety and bounds)
Active content (`script`, `foreignObject`, `iframe`, `on*`), any reference outside the document (`href`, `url(...)`, CSS `@`/`url(` in `<style>`), entities, malformed XML/UTF-8, a declared font identity naming another set, nonfinite geometry, PNG decode errors, and every byte/node/depth/command/glyph/PNG/stop ceiling.

## Alternatives
- Keep the closed subset: rejected. Agents could not hand-write a circle.
- Add resvg/usvg as a second, permissive renderer: rejected. It adds a renderer and a dependency, where one permissive parser over the existing painter is enough.
