# Propose a bounded experiment

Subrecipe of [[recipes/autoresearch|Autoresearch]]. Turn a question and retained
evidence into a frozen test definition. A proposal is not execution authority.

## Inputs

The program purpose, governing decisions/spec/invariants, exact recipe revisions,
environment manifest/capabilities, previous findings, source/data inventories,
partition history and remaining budget.

## Steps

1. Read relevant research, failed attempts and decisions. State the question,
   changed premise and what observation could disprove the proposed explanation.
   Rephrasing an old idea does not create independent evidence.
2. Choose inspection, one-shot construction, intervention/comparison or replication.
   Declare observable predictions for inspection, or candidate/reference artifacts
   for comparisons. Code, prompts, datasets and numerical/model artifacts are valid
   surfaces; identify what can change and what stays fixed.
3. Freeze the evaluator/confirmation procedure, units, controls, sample/seed rule,
   validity conditions and decision criterion. An evaluator redesign starts a new
   evaluation version. Do not optimize against withheld confirmation data.
4. Bind exact source/input/artifact and environment identities. Identify intended
   input use and every previous exposure. An inspected holdout becomes development
   evidence. Record missing outcomes and coverage instead of substituting zero.
5. Allocate bounded attempts/arms/probes and per-step/program resource ceilings
   within the parent allowance. Declare required effect/isolation capabilities,
   retry/expiry rules and stop/review conditions. Missing usage is not free.
6. Run or reuse the applicable pre-admission invariant witnesses, with exact source
   bindings and actual exits. For new stateful implementation, require the relevant
   formal model, broken controls and implementation witness before source acceptance.
7. Create the experiment definition through the selected supported KB route, or
   retain its admitted immutable artifact under the declared schema. Link parent,
   recipe/governance closure, question, inputs, environment, evaluator and bounds.
   Record unsupported record/evidence capabilities rather than bypassing the KB.

## Outputs and verification

Return a unique experiment ID and immutable plan, its source/recipe/governance
references, required check evidence and a clear admitted/not-admitted disposition.
Verification checks complete bindings and supported capabilities, not whether the
idea sounds promising. Discovery can end with no experiment selected.

## Failure and recovery

Retain the failed premise, missing requirement or contradictory evidence and the
condition for a useful revisit. A plan change creates a new revision/attempt;
never retrospectively preregister an old result or overwrite a frozen definition.
Resolve original uncertain KB operations before retrying a write.
