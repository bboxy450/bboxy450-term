Canonical source: https://github.com/bboxy450/bboxy450-term

Policy: https://github.com/bboxy450/bboxy450-term/blob/main/ATTRIBUTION.md

Slop respectfully. Keep the credit attached. Retain origin, copyright and
license notices when reusing code or designs. Describe downstream reuse
accurately as “Copied from”, “Adapted from”, “Translated from”, or “Inspired by”
bboxy450-term, with a link to the source. Independent inspiration credit is a
request, not an additional license condition.
